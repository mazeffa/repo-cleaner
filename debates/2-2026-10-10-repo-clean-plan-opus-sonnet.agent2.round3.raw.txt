## Context

repo-clean is a Claude Code plugin (this repo, release 0.9.0, commit `34afc13`) that keeps a
project's docs authoritative so Claude can resume work. D-017 is the measure: someone opens a
project untouched for months and is working within a minute from what the SessionStart hook
prints (the log's last `State:`/`Next:`). Pieces: hooks `hooks/session_start.py`,
`hooks/post_tool_use.py`, `hooks/stop.py` (wired in `hooks/hooks.json`), fold-in helper
`hooks/fold.py` (run by Claude per `skills/repo-clean/maintain.md`), shared `hooks/_lib.py`,
checker `skills/repo-clean/scripts/check_docs.py` (1353 lines: ~930 checker + ~420 embedded
`--selfcheck` fixture, 10 rules), test suite `hooks/selftest.py`.

This debate continues `debates/1-2026-10-10-repo-clean-to-9-of-10.md` (four agents, one round;
read it for the full history and line-level evidence). Everything agreed there is folded into
the Proposal below as the starting text; what was not agreed is in the Dispute Log as `D1` rows.
Sonnet's round-1 turn in debate 1 was rejected by the orchestrator on a formatting rule, not on
content; its findings (E11, E12) are carried here.
